## Overview

The competition theme was "Agents that Innovate Daily Life." Starting from negotiation agents, we narrowed the idea down to second-hand markets, and then to agents that bridge information asymmetry in peer-to-peer trading.

## How it works

The system adapts [NegotiationArena](https://github.com/vinid/NegotiationArena), a framework for evaluating negotiation agents. Two standalone LLM agents negotiate with each other, and a rule-based validator checks the process for safety and reliability.
